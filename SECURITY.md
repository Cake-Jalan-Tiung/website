# Security

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report security problems privately through GitHub: open the **Security** tab of this repository and choose **Report a vulnerability**. Do not open a public issue.

Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and what it lets someone do. You will get a reply within a few working days.

Please do not test against the live site in ways that place real orders, take payments, or lock staff out.

## Scope

This repository is the Cake Jalan Tiung online shop: the storefront, checkout, CHIP payment callback, staff screen and the StoreHub sales sync. Problems in CHIP, StoreHub or Cloudflare themselves belong with those providers.
